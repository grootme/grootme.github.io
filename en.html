Senior Fullstack Developer with 13+ years of experience (2013-2026) in designing, developing, and integrating enterprise ERP solutions (Odoo, ERPNext, Distra ERP) and multi-industry web platforms. Architect of enterprise monorepos: experience (Flutter/Dart for mobile e-commerce, commerce platforms, corporate web, enterprise social network) and enterprise (Java Spring Boot for ERP and corporate services). Creator of Ricco ID, an identity provider for individuals and businesses. Specialized in monolithic and microservices architectures, MCP development for persistent solutions, and integrations with AI agent platforms and BPMN process management (ProcessMaker). Proficient in containerization (Docker/Kubernetes), observability (Grafana/Prometheus, ELK Stack), automation (n8n, Flowise, LangGraph), payment gateway integration, and blockchain (SmartContracts/Web3.js). Databases and messaging: MongoDB, Qdrant, PgVector, TimescaleDB, Kafka, RabbitMQ, Redis. Distributed storage with Minio S3. GitFlow methodology. Experience with WSO2 IS/Keycloak, mono-repo architectures, and cloud (AWS, Hostinger VPS).

Development of MCP (Model Context Protocol) for persistent solutions, enabling stateful communication between AI agents and enterprise services. Design on monolithic or microservices architectures depending on project scale. Integrations with agentic AI platforms, automations with n8n and deployment on cloud solutions (AWS, GCP, Azure). My approach combines the solidity of enterprise development with the agility of modern technologies.
